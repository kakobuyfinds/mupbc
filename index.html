LitBuy shopping guide

Trace LitBuy product links to the seller

Open the original Weidian, Taobao or 1688 listing when available. Save the marketplace product ID, seller name, listing URL and date checked. When comparing LitBuy finds, confirm the color option, size variant and whether the listing shows a single item or a set.

If you use an agent link converter, compare the converted listing with the original. Check the product ID, photos and variant names before adding anything to your cart.

Compare sizing in centimeters

Start with the seller size chart and a garment you already own. For hoodies and jerseys, compare chest width and body length. For jackets, add shoulder width and sleeve length. For pants and shorts, check waist, rise and inseam.

For sneakers, compare your foot length with the seller’s sizing instructions. If the listing gives an insole measurement, confirm how it was taken before using it to choose a size.

Read LitBuy QC photos against the listing

Match the warehouse photos to your selected variant. Check the size label, color, stitching and visible marks. For shoes, ask for outsole and heel views if they are missing. For clothing, a flat measurement photo can help resolve a sizing mismatch.

Before approving shipment, check the return window, who pays domestic return shipping and how the agent handles a wrong item.

Build a LitBuy shipping estimate

Record the item price, domestic shipping and any service fee first. Compare parcel weight and box dimensions across available international routes. Some routes charge by volumetric weight, so a bulky shoebox can change the quote.

Use the destination country, postal code and currency when comparing quotes. Check whether the quoted total includes any destination taxes or duties.

Frequently asked questions

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference that organizes product finds and links. A row may show a category, image, variant or seller reference. Open the original listing to confirm current options before ordering.

What should I do if a product link is dead?

Look for the original marketplace listing or search for the exact model and variant. Compare the seller name and listing photos before saving a replacement link.

Can LitBuy QC photos prove authenticity?

No. Warehouse photos can help you check visible details, measurements and the selected variant. For authenticity, look for documented provenance and a seller that offers verifiable proof.

How do I estimate the total cost before ordering?

Add the item cost, domestic delivery, applicable service fees and international shipping. Check for taxes or duties at your destination and update your estimate when parcel weight and dimensions are available.
